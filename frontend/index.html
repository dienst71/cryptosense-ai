How To Place a Trade on Coinbase — Step by Step
📲 When CryptoSense sends a BUY alert:
1 Open Coinbase app → tap the coin (e.g. XRP)
2 Tap Buy → enter dollar amount (max 20% of total, e.g. $600)
3 Confirm buy at market price — you now own XRP
4 Immediately go to Advanced Trade → set a Stop Limit sell at the stop loss price shown in the alert
5 Set a Limit sell for half your coins at Take Profit 1 price
6 Set another Limit sell for the other half at Take Profit 2 price
